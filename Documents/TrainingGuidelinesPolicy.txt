TRAINING GUIDELINES
Trainees are expected to conduct themselves professionally during all training sessions and interactions.
Trainees should keep their development environment, tools, and software updated as instructed by the trainer.
Trainees should maintain organized notes and code repositories for easy reference during the program.
Any training material, sample code, or documents provided are meant for learning purposes during the program.
Trainees should communicate through the appropriate official channels for training-related queries.
Feedback on training sessions, assignments, or projects should be shared constructively through the designated feedback process.
Trainees should follow the schedule and timelines communicated by the training coordinator for sessions, assignments, and assessments.
