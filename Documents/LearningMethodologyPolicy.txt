LEARNING METHODOLOGY
The training follows a hands-on, project-based learning methodology that combines concept explanation with immediate practical application.
Each topic is generally introduced through a short theory session followed by a live coding demonstration.
Trainees are encouraged to code along during demonstrations rather than only observing.
Reference projects and sample applications are provided to help trainees understand real-world architecture and coding conventions.
Trainees are encouraged to ask questions during sessions and to revisit recorded material or notes for topics that need reinforcement.
Peer learning and discussion are encouraged, provided that individual assignment and project work remains the trainee's own.
Trainees are advised to practice concepts outside of scheduled sessions to strengthen understanding and retention.
