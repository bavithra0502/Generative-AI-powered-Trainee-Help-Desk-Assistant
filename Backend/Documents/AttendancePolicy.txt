ATTENDANCE POLICY
Trainees are expected to attend training sessions regularly.
Regular attendance is important for understanding concepts and completing practical activities.
Trainees should join training sessions on time.
If a trainee is unable to attend a session, the trainee should inform the appropriate trainer or training coordinator as early as possible.
Trainees are responsible for catching up on concepts and activities missed during an absence.
Attendance may be considered when evaluating trainee participation and overall performance.
Trainees should avoid unnecessary absences during important practical sessions, assessments, or project activities.
Repeated absence may affect a trainee's ability to complete training activities successfully.
