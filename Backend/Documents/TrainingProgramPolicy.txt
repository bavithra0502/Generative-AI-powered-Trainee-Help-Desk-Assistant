TRAINING PROGRAM POLICY
The training program is designed to provide trainees with both theoretical knowledge and practical application development experience.
Training sessions may include instructor demonstrations, coding exercises, discussions, assignments, and practical activities.
Trainees are expected to actively participate in training sessions and practical exercises.
The training program may include individual activities as well as group-based learning activities.
Trainees are encouraged to practice the concepts learned during training.
Practical application development is an important part of the learning process.
Trainees are expected to complete assigned learning activities within the specified timelines.
The training program focuses on developing technical knowledge, programming skills, problem-solving ability, and practical application development skills.
