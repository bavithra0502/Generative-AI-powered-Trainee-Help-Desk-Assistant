ASSIGNMENT POLICY
Assignments are provided to help trainees practice the concepts learned during training.
Trainees are expected to complete assignments within the specified deadline.
Assignments should be completed by the trainee unless collaboration is specifically permitted.
Trainees should understand the solution they submit.
Copying another trainee's assignment or submitting someone else's work as their own is not permitted.
Trainees should ask the trainer for clarification if they do not understand an assignment requirement.
Assignments may be reviewed to evaluate technical understanding and practical skills.
Late submission may affect assignment evaluation depending on the training guidelines.
Trainees should test their applications before submitting assignments.
