ASSESSMENT POLICY
Trainees are periodically assessed through machine tests, quizzes, assignments, and project evaluations.
Assessments are designed to evaluate both theoretical understanding and practical coding ability.
Machine tests are generally time-bound and must be completed and submitted within the given duration.
Trainees should read assessment instructions carefully before starting, including submission requirements and total marks.
The use of unauthorized help or plagiarism during an assessment is not permitted.
Assessment results may be used to identify areas where a trainee needs additional support or practice.
Trainees who are unclear about assessment expectations should reach out to the trainer or training coordinator before the assessment begins.
